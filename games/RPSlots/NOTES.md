## Design decisions

- Chosen: three machines in one image sharing one purse: LUCKY 7, SWEET and DRAGON FORTUNE. The skeleton came from CHCraps (prefix `CHSL_`).
- Chosen: LUCKY 7 is the classic: 3 reels, one line, 15 old-school symbols and a bonus wheel.
  - Arm: hold A to pull, release to spin.
  - The bonus wheel was added at the owner's request.
- Chosen: DRAGON FORTUNE has 5x3 reels and 25 lines.
  - Features in build order: free spins, wilds, jackpot meters, hold and spin. That order was the cut order if flash ran out; all four were built.
  - Its high symbol is a lucky cat.
- Chosen: SWEET, the third machine, added at the owner's request: candy theme, 3x3 reels, 5 lines, lollipop wild, sugar-rush ladder.
- Chosen: on the modern machines A spins, and A during a spin stops the reels early.
- Chosen: no dealer anywhere ("just machines").
- Chosen: over-the-top effects (coin explosions `fx::explode`, the dragon rising in fire, ticking meters), but the style must stay cohesive with the sibling games.
- Chosen: each machine's palette swaps the three felt slots:
  - FORTUNE: maroon/jade/orange; SWEET: pink/mint/lilac.
  - The classic machine has no orange or purple (orange fruit uses SKIN, plum WINE).
- Chosen in the final polish round:
  - the machine picker is a menu of rows with mascot icons (not cabinets);
  - the paytable is on B (not hold-B), a scrolling list that auto-scrolls;
  - max bet on B was removed;
  - a welcome banner when sitting down, and big-win figures under the banner;
  - the coin shows a $ with its value under it.
- All three machines are tuned to return about 95% (the README has the figures).

## Gotchas

- Device debug builds (`CHSL_LEAN`) leave out saving (stubs in `Save.cpp`), the options and stats pages, and the attract demo (`CHSL_DEMO` defaults to `!CHSL_LEAN`). `-DCHSL_FULL` forces a full device debug build.
- `tools/art/symbols.png` is the source of the reel symbols, and the owner hand-edits it (BAR, 7 and the cat so far).
  - Never delete or regenerate it from the recipes in `tools/assets.py`.
  - To add symbols, add a recipe and run `python tools/assets.py --export`: cells outside the sheet fall back to their recipe, and existing cells are kept.
  - Rows: LUCKY 7, then FORTUNE, then SWEET, each on its own machine's palette (`tools/art/palette.png`/`.gpl`).
- Reel strips are generated by `tools/strips.py` (fixed seed). After changing symbol counts or pays, regenerate them, rerun `chgame test` and update the README's return figures.
- The drawing, lettering, palette, fx maths and input are the CHGame library's (`<CHGame.h>`, `platform/board/arduino/CHGame/libraries/CHGame`); the game's own felts are `mach::THEMES` (`Machine.cpp`), given to `pal::setThemes()` in `setup()`. The library's `text35` honours `gfx_setClip`, but the reel window and the paytable still draw a text only when it is wholly inside, as they did when the game's own `text35` clipped only to the screen.
- Music is three `audio::Melody` tunes in `Sounds.cpp` (Title, Free, Wheel), one line of notes looping under the effects, played by the CHGame library; `playSong()` and `setMusicOn()` (the Options MUSIC toggle) are the game's. Preview the tunes and effects with `chgame audio out/audio`.
- The play screen redraws in three bands (top, reels, bottom). `chgame redraw tools/scripts/diff/diff.txt out/diff` catches stale pixels (`chgame check` runs it); rerun it after touching any redraw.
- Debug hooks are sent with `say`; the list is in `Screens.cpp`:
  - `R` seed, `F` force stops, `G n` force a feature (1 free games, 2 hold and spin, 3 dragon, 4 five cats, 5 LUCKY 7 wheel);
  - `J` screen (T M C F Y O S W L), `M` purse, `E` bet index, `V` reboot from flash, `H` state;
  - `Q` calibration (simulator only).
- Compiler: the simulator and tests need `CHSIM_CXX` set, or zig/clang++/g++ on PATH (see the root CLAUDE.md).

## Development

Everything can be checked on a PC (Python 3 with Pillow, the repository's `tools/requirements.txt`, and a C++ compiler for the simulator and tests: root CLAUDE.md). `chgame check` runs it all.

    chgame test     # the rules: LUCKY 7's exact return, SWEET and DRAGON FORTUNE by Monte Carlo, money conservation
    chgame sim
    chgame run tools/scripts/<name>.txt out/<name>    # a script in the simulator: screenshots and GIFs
    chgame redraw tools/scripts/diff/diff.txt out/diff     # stale-pixel check of the band redraw
    chgame gif    # tools/scripts/gameplay.txt -> docs/gameplay.gif (the README's one GIF, <= 1 MB)
    python tools/assets.py [--export]   # art -> src/assets, or write the sheet and palette
    python tools/strips.py              # regenerate the reel strips (src/game/Strips.h)
    chgame audio out/audio    # the sound effects and tunes as WAV
    chgame build [--debug]             # compile for the board, with the size
    chgame upload       # build and upload the release

- Scripts (`tools/scripts`): the shared commands (`wait`, `tap`, `hold`, `release`, `snap`, `rec`, `say`, `perf`, `cal`) plus this game's `idle [W]`, which runs until the spin and everything it set off has been shown, then W frames more. The `say` hooks are listed under Gotchas.
  - `gameplay.txt` records the README's clips: the title, three sevens on LUCKY 7, the bonus wheel, the dragon, and SWEET's sugar rush. The sevens are played at $1 (`say E 0`): at $5 the 250x passes the $1000 goal and the clip ends on YOU BROKE THE BANK. `say M 500` puts the purse back between clips for the same reason.
  - `showcase.txt` and `showcase_sweet.txt` walk the three machines through their features and paytables (run them into `out/`); `screens.txt` snaps every screen once; `save.txt` checks save, continue and the demo; `perf.txt` prints the frame-time estimates; `diff/diff.txt` is for `chgame redraw`.
- The art: the reel symbols are one sprite sheet, `tools/art/symbols.png`, in 22x22 cells: LUCKY 7's fifteen on the top row, DRAGON FORTUNE's ten on the second, SWEET's eight on the third. Every opaque pixel must be exactly a palette colour (`tools/art/palette.png`, `palette.gpl`); the second and third rows use their machines' palettes. Then `python tools/assets.py`.
- Building by hand: Arduino IDE with *Tools > Optimize > Smallest + LTO* and *Tools > USB > Upload only* (the game does not use USB Serial), or `arduino-cli compile -b CHGame:ch32v:rev0:opt=oslto,rtlib=nano,periph=game,usb=uploadonly` (the board package 0.3.0 on brings the CHGfx and CHGame libraries). `chgame build` does the same with the repository's copies.
- It shares its palette, lettering, banners and coin fountains with CHBlackjack, CHChess, CHCraps, CHRoulette and the rest of the series.
- Files:

      CHSlots.ino          the frame loop
      config.h             build switches
      Slots.*              the rules of the three machines
      Presenter.*          the show; Fx.* is chgame/Sizzle configured plus explode()
      Machine.*, Layout.h  the cabinets, reels, wheel, paytables, each machine's felt
      Screens.*            title, machine menu, play, options, stats, endings
      Sounds.*             sound effects and three tunes (the CHGame library plays them)
      Save.*               what a save holds (the CHGame library keeps it in flash)
      src/game/Strips.h    the reel strips (generated by tools/strips.py)
      src/assets/          generated art (tools/assets.py)
      tools/               art, asset and strip generators, simulator, tests
